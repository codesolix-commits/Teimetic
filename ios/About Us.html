About Teimetic

iOS version · Built by Code Solix

Teimetic is a personal screen-time companion for iPhone and iPad that helps you build healthier digital habits through focus sessions, optional rules, and clear insights.

Our Mission

Technology should serve you. Teimetic gives you simple tools to stay intentional with your time.

iOS Features

  • Focus timers with history
  • Customizable rules and goals
  • Optional notifications
  • Sample usage insights (real Screen Time access not used in the current version)
  • Clean, modern interface

Privacy

Focus history and preferences stay on your device. The current iOS version does not access real Screen Time data. See the iOS Privacy Policy for full details.
