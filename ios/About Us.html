About Snoleep

iOS · Sleep Tracking & Wellness

Snoleep is a sleep tracking and wellness application designed to help you understand your sleep patterns, review your sleep history, explore trends, monitor estimated snoring activity, and create a more comfortable bedtime routine.

Features

  • Sleep tracking and sleep history.
  • Sleep scores and sleep-stage estimates.
  • Weekly, monthly, and historical insights.
  • Sleep schedules and alarm-related features.
  • Snoring detection and overnight recordings.
  • Sleep sounds and custom sound mixes.
  • AI Chat for general sleep and wellness information.
  • Premium analytics and extended history.
  • Premium AI-powered sleep assistance and insights.

Our Approach

Snoleep is designed to make sleep information easier to understand through simple summaries, wellness estimates, trends, and practical sleep tools.

Snoleep is intended for general wellness and informational purposes only and is not a replacement for professional medical advice.

Important Wellness Disclaimer

Snoleep is a wellness and sleep-tracking application, not a medical device.

Snoleep does not diagnose or treat sleep disorders, sleep apnea, or any other medical condition.
