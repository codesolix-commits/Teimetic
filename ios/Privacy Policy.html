Privacy Policy

Teimetic for iOS · Last updated: October 31, 2026

Teimetic (“we”, “our”, or “the App”) is a personal screen-time management application developed by Code Solix for iPhone and iPad. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Important: The current iOS version of Teimetic operates in sample-data mode for usage statistics. It does not access your real Screen Time data unless and until Apple Screen Time / Family Controls entitlements are obtained and this policy is updated.

1. Information We Access on iOS

  • Notifications – If you allow notifications, the App can send local alerts when a limit is reached or a focus session ends. This uses the standard iOS notification permission.
  • Focus session history – Label, duration and timestamp of sessions you complete are stored locally on your device.
  • Rules & preferences – Daily goals, rules, theme and notification settings are stored locally.
  • Purchase status – A local flag indicating whether you have an active Teimetic Premium subscription. Payment processing is handled entirely by Apple.
  • Sample / demo usage data – The App may display illustrative sample statistics so you can explore the interface. This is not your real usage data.

2. How We Use the Information

  • To run focus timers and keep a local history of completed sessions.
  • To remember the rules and preferences you set.
  • To send optional local notifications you enable.
  • To show sample insights while real Screen Time access is not available.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage & Sharing

  • Focus history, rules and preferences are stored locally on your iOS device.
  • We do not operate a server that receives continuous personal usage history from the iOS app.
  • If you contact support by email, the information you choose to send is used only to respond to your request.
  • Subscription payments are processed by Apple. We never receive your full payment card details.

4. Permissions Summary (iOS)

  • Notifications – Optional. Can be denied or later disabled in iOS Settings → Notifications → Teimetic.
  • The App does not currently request or use the Family Controls / Screen Time entitlement. If this changes in a future version, we will update this Privacy Policy and the App Store disclosure before collecting real usage data.

5. Children’s Privacy

Teimetic is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Your Choices

  • Disable notifications in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Delete focus history and rules from within the App where the option is provided.
  • Uninstalling the App removes locally stored data from that device.

7. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
