Privacy Policy

Snoleep (iOS) · Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Snoleep handles information when you use the Snoleep iOS application.

1. Information We Collect

Snoleep is designed to provide sleep tracking and wellness features without requiring an account for standard use.

  • Sleep information: Sleep duration, sleep history, sleep stages, sleep scores, schedules, trends, and related information may be stored on your device.
  • Snoring information: Snoleep may process audio recordings and detected snoring events when you choose to use snoring detection and recording.
  • Audio processing: Snore detection audio is processed on the device where supported by the application. Snoring detection is an automated estimate and is not a medical diagnosis.
  • AI features: If you choose to use AI Chat or another AI-powered feature, information you submit may be processed to generate the requested response. Supported features may allow you to choose whether your sleep information is used as context.
  • App preferences: Settings such as appearance, sleep preferences, sound preferences, schedules, and other application settings may be stored locally.
  • Purchases: Premium subscriptions and purchases are processed through Apple's App Store in-app purchase system.

2. Permissions

Snoleep may request access to the microphone when you choose features that require audio recording or snoring detection.

You can manage permissions through your iOS device settings.

3. How We Use Information

  • Track and display sleep information.
  • Provide sleep scores and wellness estimates.
  • Display sleep history, trends, and insights.
  • Provide snoring detection and recordings.
  • Provide sleep sounds and related audio features.
  • Provide AI-powered wellness information when requested.
  • Save application preferences.
  • Enable premium functionality after purchase.

4. Data Storage

Snoleep is designed to keep relevant application information on your device where the feature operates locally.

Snoleep does not require an account for standard use. Information stored locally may remain on your device until you delete the application or remove the relevant information.

When you voluntarily use a feature that requires information to be processed by an external service, the information necessary to provide that feature may be processed for that purpose.

5. Apple App Store and Purchases

Payments for Snoleep premium subscriptions and purchases on iOS are processed by Apple through the App Store.

Apple handles payment information according to Apple's applicable terms and privacy policies. Snoleep does not receive or store your complete payment card information through the application.

6. Subscriptions

Snoleep may offer auto-renewable subscriptions and/or other premium purchase options.

Subscription prices, billing period, free trial or introductory offers, and renewal terms are displayed before purchase.

App Store subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled through your Apple Account subscription settings.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party services when necessary to provide a feature you voluntarily use, process App Store transactions, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

Snoleep is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the application and its functionality to protect information handled by Snoleep. However, no electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Your Choices

You can control application permissions through iOS settings and may choose whether to use optional features such as snoring detection and AI Chat.

You may stop using Snoleep and delete the application at any time.

11. Wellness and Medical Disclaimer

Important: Snoleep is a wellness and sleep-tracking application. Sleep scores, sleep-stage information, snoring detection, trends, insights, AI responses, and other information are estimates intended for general informational and wellness purposes only.

Snoleep is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, prevention, or professional medical advice.

Snoring detection is an automated estimate and should not be relied upon to diagnose sleep apnea or another medical condition.

AI-generated information may be incomplete, inaccurate, or inappropriate for your individual circumstances and should not be treated as medical advice.

If you have concerns about your sleep, snoring, breathing, symptoms, or health, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

12. Disclaimer

Snoleep is provided on an "AS IS" and "AS AVAILABLE" basis. Sleep tracking results, recordings, scores, estimates, insights, sounds, and AI-generated information may not always be accurate or available.

We are not responsible for inaccuracies in sleep estimates, missed or incorrectly detected snoring events, recording failures, device limitations, data loss, interruptions, or compatibility issues.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be published through the applicable Snoleep privacy policy page.
