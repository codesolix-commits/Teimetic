About Snoleep

Android · Sleep Tracking & Wellness

Snoleep is a sleep tracking and wellness application designed to help you understand your sleep patterns, review sleep history, explore trends, monitor estimated snoring activity, and build a more comfortable bedtime routine.

Features

  • Sleep tracking and sleep history.
  • Sleep scores and sleep-stage estimates.
  • Weekly, monthly, and historical sleep insights.
  • Sleep schedule and alarm features.
  • Snoring detection and overnight recordings.
  • Sleep sounds and custom sound mixes.
  • AI Chat for general sleep and wellness information.
  • Premium analytics and extended history.
  • Premium AI-powered sleep assistance and insights.

Our Approach

Snoleep is designed to make sleep information easier to understand through simple summaries, trends, wellness estimates, and practical tools.

The information provided by Snoleep is intended for general wellness and informational purposes and is not intended to replace professional medical advice.
