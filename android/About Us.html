About Teimetic

Android version · Built by Code Solix

Teimetic is a personal screen-time companion for Android that helps you understand where your time goes and build healthier digital habits.

Our Mission

Technology should serve you. Teimetic gives clear insights, simple rules and focused sessions so you can spend more time on what matters.

Android Features

  • Real app-usage tracking when you grant Usage Access
  • Daily goals and per-app rules
  • Focus timers with history
  • Optional limit and session notifications
  • Clean, modern interface

Privacy

Usage data is processed on your device. See the Android Privacy Policy for full details about the Usage Access permission and how your information is handled.
