Privacy Policy

Teimetic for Android · Last updated: October 31, 2026

Teimetic (“we”, “our”, or “the App”) is a personal screen-time management application developed by Code Solix for Android devices. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Short summary: On Android, Teimetic can read your app usage only after you manually grant the special “Usage Access” permission. Your detailed usage data is processed on your device. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information We Access on Android

  • Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS) – This is a special Android permission. When you grant it in system Settings, the App can read how long individual apps have been used. This data is used only to show you personal insights, apply the limits you set, and trigger optional notifications. You can revoke this permission at any time.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) – Optional. Used to send local alerts when a daily or app limit is reached or when a focus session ends.
  • Focus session history – Label, duration and timestamp of sessions you complete are stored locally on your device.
  • Rules & preferences – Daily goals, app rules, theme and notification settings are stored locally.
  • Purchase status – A local flag indicating whether you have an active Teimetic Premium subscription. Payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play.

2. How We Use the Information

  • To display your personal screen-time statistics and trends.
  • To enforce the daily limits and app rules you create.
  • To run focus timers and keep a local history of completed sessions.
  • To send optional local notifications about limits and sessions.

We do not use your usage data for advertising, profiling, cross-app tracking, or selling to third parties.

3. Data Storage & Sharing

  • Detailed usage statistics, rules and focus history are stored locally on your Android device.
  • We do not operate a server that continuously receives your app-usage history.
  • If you contact support by email, the information you choose to send is used only to respond to your request.
  • Subscription payments are processed by Google Play. We never receive your full payment card details.

4. Permissions Summary (Android)

  • Usage Access – Required for real tracking. Must be enabled manually in Android Settings → Special app access → Usage access. Can be turned off at any time.
  • Notifications – Optional. Can be denied or later disabled in system settings.
  • Internet – Used only for opening support links, store pages, and (if applicable) restoring purchases.

5. Children’s Privacy

Teimetic is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information and we will delete it.

6. Your Choices

  • Revoke Usage Access or notification permission in Android system settings at any time.
  • Delete focus history and rules from within the App where the option is provided.
  • Uninstalling the App removes locally stored data from that device.

7. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
