Privacy Policy

Snoleep (Android) · Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Snoleep handles information when you use the Snoleep Android application.

1. Information We Collect

Snoleep is designed to provide sleep tracking and wellness features without requiring you to create an account for standard use.

  • Sleep information: Sleep duration, sleep history, sleep stages, sleep scores, schedules, trends, and related information may be stored on your device so that Snoleep can provide sleep tracking and insights.
  • Snoring information: Snoleep may process audio recordings or detected snoring events when you use the snoring detection and recording features.
  • Audio processing: Snore detection audio is processed on the device where supported by the application. Snore detection is an automated estimate and is not a medical diagnosis.
  • AI features: If you use Snoleep's AI Chat or AI-powered wellness features, information you choose to submit may be processed to generate responses. You may choose whether to use your available sleep information with supported AI features.
  • App settings: Preferences such as appearance, sleep settings, sound settings, schedules, and other application preferences may be stored locally.
  • Purchases: Premium subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. Purchase status may be used to enable premium features.

2. Permissions

Snoleep may request permissions that are necessary for features you choose to use, such as microphone access for snoring detection and recording.

Permissions are used for the corresponding application functionality. You can manage permissions through your Android device settings.

3. How We Use Information

Information handled by Snoleep may be used to:

  • Track and display your sleep information.
  • Calculate wellness-oriented sleep scores and summaries.
  • Display sleep history, trends, and insights.
  • Provide snoring detection and recording functionality.
  • Provide sleep sounds and audio-related features.
  • Provide AI-powered wellness information when you choose to use it.
  • Remember your application preferences and settings.
  • Enable premium features after a valid purchase.

4. Data Storage

Snoleep is designed to keep application information on your device where the relevant feature operates locally.

Snoleep does not require an account for standard use. Your sleep history, settings, and locally generated information may remain on your device unless you delete the application or otherwise remove the relevant data.

If you voluntarily use a feature that sends information to a service, including an AI-powered feature, the information required by that feature may be processed by that service to provide the requested functionality.

5. Third-Party Services

Payments for premium subscriptions and purchases on Android are processed by Google Play. Google may process payment and transaction information according to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Snoleep does not receive or store your complete payment card information through the application.

6. Subscriptions and Premium Features

Snoleep may offer premium features through subscriptions or one-time purchases. Available plans, prices, billing periods, trials, and other purchase terms are displayed before purchase.

Android subscriptions purchased through Google Play are managed through Google Play. Uninstalling Snoleep does not automatically cancel a subscription.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party services only when necessary to provide a feature you choose to use, process an App Store or Google Play transaction, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

Snoleep is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the application and its functionality to protect information handled by Snoleep. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Your Choices

You can control permissions through Android settings and can choose whether to use optional features such as snoring detection and AI Chat.

You may also stop using Snoleep or delete the application at any time.

11. Wellness and Medical Disclaimer

Important: Snoleep is a wellness and sleep-tracking application. Sleep scores, sleep-stage information, snoring detection, trends, insights, AI responses, and other information provided by Snoleep are estimates intended for general informational and wellness purposes only.

Snoleep is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, prevention, or professional medical advice.

Snoring detection is an automated estimate and should not be relied upon to diagnose sleep apnea or any other medical condition.

AI-generated information may be incomplete, inaccurate, or inappropriate for your individual circumstances and should not be treated as medical advice.

If you have concerns about your sleep, snoring, breathing, health, or symptoms, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

12. Disclaimer

Snoleep is provided on an "AS IS" and "AS AVAILABLE" basis. Sleep tracking results, recordings, scores, estimates, insights, sounds, and AI-generated information may not always be accurate or available.

We are not responsible for inaccuracies in sleep estimates, missed or incorrectly detected snoring events, interruptions, device limitations, recording failures, data loss, or compatibility issues.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published through the appropriate Snoleep privacy policy page.

Your continued use of Snoleep after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy where permitted by applicable law.
